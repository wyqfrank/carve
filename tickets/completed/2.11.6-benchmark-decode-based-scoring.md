- Phase 2 (decode scoring)

## Deliverables

- benchmark doc (update your existing one or new file)

  entropy-only ranking and decode-aware ranking
- The benchmark covers:
  clean Canon IXUS fixtures and the `missing_soi.JPG` corrupted fixture path
- The output table now documents before/after comparisons with:
  entropy best offset/score, decode best offset/score, decode usage, and per-candidate score gain
- Aggregate output summarizes:
  average entropy score, average decode score, decode usage rate, and separate clean/corrupted score deltas
- Key findings are printed with the benchmark output so results are documented at execution time
